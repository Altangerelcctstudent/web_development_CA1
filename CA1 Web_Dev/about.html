At "AG Travel Agency", we invite you to discover the raw beauty and ancient traditions of Mongolia. Specializing in immersive journeys through Mongolia’s vast steppes, rugged mountains, and untouched wilderness, we offer travelers a rare opportunity to experience life alongside nomadic herders and connect with a culture unchanged for centuries. Our tours are thoughtfully designed to take you off the beaten path—whether you’re riding horseback across the open plains, sleeping in traditional gers under star-filled skies, or sharing meals with local families. With deep respect for nature and tradition, we partner directly with nomadic communities to ensure authentic and responsible travel experiences.If you seek adventure, serenity, and cultural depth, AG Travel is your gateway to Mongolia’s wild soul. Come as a traveler—leave as part of a story.
